Office Recorder Alerts Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Purpose

Office Recorder Alerts is a privately operated desktop application. It processes local camera video and audio, detects motion, and sends authorized email notifications to an operator-configured recipient.

Information processed

The application processes local recordings, selected snapshots, motion timestamps, the configured recipient address, and delivery records. Google OAuth authorization credentials are stored locally so the application can send notifications through Gmail.

Google account access

The application requests only the Gmail send permission (gmail.send). It does not request permission to read mailbox messages, delete messages, or access contacts.

Google account authorization is used solely to send the operator's authorized notifications. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Storage and sharing

Recordings, snapshots, credentials, and delivery records are stored on the operator's computer. Notification text and selected snapshot attachments are transmitted to Google for Gmail delivery and shared with the configured recipient and the services handling that email.

This public information website does not host recordings, snapshots, or authorization credentials.

Retention and deletion

Local recordings, snapshots, credentials, and delivery records remain until removed by the operator or an operator-configured retention process. Sent emails and received copies remain in their respective mailboxes until deleted under those accounts' settings.

Operator controls

The operator can turn notifications off, stop recording, and revoke the application's Google account access through Google account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing local files or previously delivered emails.

Website hosting

This information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website visitor information under its own privacy statement . This website adds no analytics scripts or tracking forms.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact asgoodsoldiers@gmail.com.
