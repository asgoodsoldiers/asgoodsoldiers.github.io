Office Recorder Alerts

Office Recorder Alerts is a privately operated desktop application that records local camera video and audio, detects motion, and sends notifications to an operator-configured email address.

Gmail access

The application requests Gmail send-only permission to send motion notifications, which may include a snapshot attachment. It does not request permission to read the mailbox, delete messages, or access contacts.

Recordings and authorization credentials are stored locally on the operator's computer. Selected snapshots are transmitted through Gmail when notifications are authorized.
